Review workflow
  1. Select a slide and inspect its WSI with the predicted tile overlay.
  2. Choose cortex or medulla, then draw a region. Each completed region saves immediately to persistent storage.
  3. Adjust each class's score filter to concentrate on the tiles you want to review.
  4. Mark the slide reviewed. Start another pass whenever you want a separate review history.
  5. Retrain from human labels and original training tiles, then choose the new model and predict a slide again.
